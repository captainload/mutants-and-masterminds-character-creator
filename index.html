⚖️ Power Level Limits & Defense Caps
PL 10 Legal

In M&M 3E, defense scores are balanced in pairs where the sum of each pair cannot exceed 2 × Power Level. You may trade off values within each pair (e.g. higher Dodge with lower Toughness) as long as their total meets the cap.
